# Authoritative Software

Software authority, explicitly asserted, independently evaluated.

Who makes which claims about this software? AUTHORITY.md lets an operator declare its claims and their scope. Consumers independently decide whether to rely on them. It applies to open-source and enterprise software, whether produced with agents or without them.

AUTHORITY.md makes explicit who stands behind which artifacts and on what terms, alongside [SECURITY.md policies, SBOM inventories, SLSA provenance, Sigstore signatures and applicable CRA obligations](related-practices.md).

AUTHORITY.md is a draft proposal. It offers a way to discuss commitments and decisions; it does not certify the answers.
